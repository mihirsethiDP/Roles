Start here

The prototype at mihirsethidp.github.io/Roles is a full working model of the new roles & permissions system — every screen, rule, and guardrail is real and clickable. Nothing you do is saved permanently: refresh the page and everything resets to the seed data, so click anything without fear.

One rule runs the whole system, on every screen: what a person can actually do at a plant = their permission AND that plant's licensed modules. When something is greyed out or a save is blocked, it's this rule (or one of its guardrails) doing its job.

It ships with seed data: 11 plants across 4 companies, 5 people (Asha, Vikram, Ramesh, Priya, Kavita), and a full set of modules, roles, and licences — enough to try every flow below.

Choose who you are

At the bottom of the left rail there are three persona buttons. Everything on every screen re-scopes instantly when you switch — this is how you experience the product as three different kinds of admin:

PersonaScopeWhat's different
🌐 Company admin (DigitalPaani, Global)EverythingSees all plants and people. The only one who can toggle module licences, add plants, and hand out the Global grant.
🏢 Cluster admin (GreenGrid)The 4 GreenGrid plantsManages only GreenGrid's people and plants. Module matrix is read-only. Gets the Control panel as a home screen.
🏭 Plant admin (STP — Sector 62)One plantManages one roster. Everything else is filtered down to that plant. Also gets the Control panel.

Tip: whenever a button seems missing, check which persona you're in — most "where did it go?" moments are scoping working correctly.

ScreenPeople (User Center) landing screen

The person registry everything drives from. Top: KPI counts and a needs-attention queue that counts down as you fix things (missing reasons, people with zero access). Below: the directory table — every person with their per-plant role chips, admin grant, and status badges.

  • Search name or title… and the filter dropdowns narrow the table.
  • ＋ Add person creates someone new — they start with zero access until you assign plants.
  • Click any row to open the profile editor — that's where all assignment work happens (see Recipe 1).
  • Below the directory: the Plant-wide actions panel (bulk changes, Recipe 6) and the custom-role apply flow (Recipe 5).

ScreenPlants

The plant registry, grouped by company label, with licensed-module chips, headcount, and the roster mix per plant.

  • Click a plant row to open its record: licensed modules, the full roster (click a person to jump to their profile), and roster editing (Recipe 8).
  • ＋ Add plant (Company admin only): name + company label. New plants start with Platform Core only — licences are switched on in Product modules.
  • The modal's ✏️ Edit on Product modules → button jumps you to that plant's row in the module matrix and highlights it.

ScreenProduct modules

The licensing ceiling — the one place module flags are written. Top: a card per module (click one for its detail: what it unlocks, how many plants have it). Bottom: the plant × module matrix with a switch per cell.

  • Switches work only as the Company admin persona — cluster/plant admins see the matrix read-only with a 🔒 banner. That's the model, not a bug.
  • Toggling a module is the best "what if" in the prototype: every other screen updates instantly (see Recipe 3).
  • Floc Detector (BioHealthTrack) legitimately unlocks nothing — it's a hardware entitlement that tracks what was sold.

ScreenRole library

The full catalog: five base roles and four admin grants as cards (click any card for its complete brief — every permission it carries, who holds it), plus the custom-role register below.

  • Custom role = a named add/remove template, not a new kind of role. Creating one does nothing by itself — you apply it to chosen people later (Recipe 5).
  • Each custom role in the register shows its grants/revokes, reason, creator, and where it's been applied. Retire archives the template without touching anyone who already received it.
  • The permission palette when building one is scope-aware: Company admin picks from everything; a cluster/plant admin only sees permissions their licensed modules unlock.

ScreenAccess review verify

The auditor's screen. Two lenses, toggle at the top:

  • 👤 Person lens — one person's capability × plant grid. Five cell states: ✓ default · ✚ added · ⊘ removed · ▢ capped by module · − off. Click any cell for the why-chain — a one-sentence explanation of exactly why that cell is what it is.
  • Plant lens — one plant's roster, the capability query ("who can do this here?" — it respects the module ceiling, so it never lists someone whose ability is capped), and Changed recently, the audit trail a reviewer checks first.

Colour rule you can trust everywhere: amber always means a deviation from standard; grey always means the module isn't licensed at that plant.

ScreenUI previews verify

A live mock of what the actual product shows each kind of user — computed from permission state by the same rules the real frontend will use, not hand-drawn per role.

  • Pick a preset (each role, each grant) or ◉ Selected person — which renders whoever you currently have open in the People tab, including their exceptions.
  • Change the plant context to apply that plant's module ceiling to the preview — the same person looks different at a plant with fewer modules.
  • The why-list under the mock explains every tab, button, and banner: which permission put it there.

ScreenControl panel cluster / plant personas only

The scoped home for the two smaller personas: their scope's key numbers, quick actions (plant-wide change, apply custom role), a "who can do X here?" lookup, and their roster with jump-to-manage. As Company admin you won't see it — Global lands on People instead.

Recipes

Each recipe starts from the persona named in step 1 and assumes a fresh page (refresh first if unsure).

1Add a new person and give them access

  1. As Company admin, open People and press ＋ Add person. Give a name and title, then Add & open.
  2. Their profile opens with zero access. In the plant picker, click each plant they should reach — plants are grouped by company label.
  3. Pick their role (the tier buttons). The disclosure below shows exactly which permission sets that role carries, and 📦 markers show anything the selected plants' modules would cap.
  4. Administering people or plant config? Open the Admin duties disclosure and pick one grant. Otherwise skip it.
  5. Press Save assignments. If anything deviates from the role standard, the save button counts the missing reasons and stays blocked until each deviation has one.
  6. The save confirmation shows what was written; the exact JSON payload is behind the For engineers disclosure. The change also lands in the audit trail.

2Grant remote control to one person at one plant

  1. Open the person from People (try Asha — she already has this grant at STP — Sector 62, so you can see the end state).
  2. On the plant row where they need it, expand the permission sets and switch on IoT remote control → actuate.
  3. The row turns amber and the save blocks until you write a reason — certification reference, approver, ticket number.
  4. Save assignments. Note what you can't do: remote control never appears in custom roles or bulk actions — one person, one plant, one reason is the only path. That's by design.

3Simulate a contract change (module ceiling in action)

  1. As Company admin, open Access review, person lens, and pick someone with several plants (Priya). Note which cells are live.
  2. Go to Product modules and switch off a module at one of her plants — say Dashboards & Analytics at STP — Sitapura.
  3. Return to Access review: the analytics cells at that plant are now ▢ grey ("capped"). Click one — the why-chain names the missing module.
  4. Switch the module back on: the cells come back exactly as they were. The ceiling caps but never edits — nothing was deleted from her profile.

4Answer "who can approve issues at this plant?"

  1. Open Access review and switch to the plant lens.
  2. Pick the plant, then use the capability query and choose the approval permission.
  3. The list shows everyone who can actually do it there — and how they got it (role standard vs exception). If the ops module is off at that plant, the honest answer is "nobody", and that's what it shows.
  4. (As a cluster/plant admin, the same lookup lives on the Control panel.)

5Create a custom role and apply it

  1. Open Role library → create custom role. Name it for the situation (e.g. "Contract-X Inventory Auditor"), tick permissions to add and/or remove, write the reason. Creating it changes nobody.
  2. Go to People → the apply panel. Pick the custom role, the people, and the plants (Company admin gets ⚡ whole-cluster chips).
  3. Choose the mode: add layers it on top of what each person has; overwrite resets them to their role standard first.
  4. Read the per-person preview — it shows exactly what will change for each person, including things they already had and things the module ceiling will cap.
  5. Write the reason and apply. Applications are stamped — editing the template later never retroactively changes anyone.

6Run a plant-wide bulk action

  1. On People, scroll to Plant-wide actions — one plant, many people, one audited action. Pick the plant.
  2. Either bulk-assign a role to the roster, or bulk add/remove one permission for ticked people.
  3. Write the shared reason (mandatory). Sensitive exceptions are skipped automatically, and anyone blocked by the module ceiling is skipped too — the summary line counts every skip. Nothing fails silently.

7Preview exactly what someone will see

  1. Open the person in People (their live config, including unsaved edits, is what gets previewed).
  2. Go to UI previews and pick ◉ Selected person.
  3. Switch the plant context between their plants — watch tabs and buttons appear/disappear as each plant's module ceiling applies.
  4. Read the why-list: every element of the mock is explained by the permission that produced it. This is the spec the real frontend follows.

8Add or remove someone from a plant's roster

  1. Open Plants and click the plant.
  2. ＋ Add person in the roster section → pick any unassigned person and a role → add. It's written immediately and audited.
  3. Remove on a roster row takes them off this plant only — their other plants are untouched.
  4. Scoping applies here too: a plant admin can only do this at their own plant.

Reading the colours & badges

SignalMeaning — always, everywhere
amberA deviation from the role standard. Every amber has (or demands) a written reason.
grey / ▢ cappedThe module isn't licensed at that plant. The permission is saved but inactive; it comes back if the plant's plan changes.
standardExactly the role standard — nothing custom.
n deviationsDrift badge — how far this person is from standard. Rising drift across many people means the role standard itself should change.
📦Capped count — abilities this person holds that a plant's modules currently switch off.
🔒Out of your persona's scope (e.g. module switches for non-Global admins, the Global grant chip).

Heads-up: the July 30 ruling

One person = one role, everywhere. On July 30 we decided a user holds a single role account-wide — a role is the person's capability level, not a per-plant attribute. The prototype hasn't been reworked yet: its profile editor still offers a role picker per plant. Until the rework lands, use it with the same role at every plant to mirror the decided model. Plant assignments (which plants someone can access), the module ceiling, per-plant reasoned exceptions like remote control, and admin grants are all unaffected by this change.
