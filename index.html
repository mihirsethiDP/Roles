The hierarchy — modules cap, roles default, exceptions flex

Each plant is licensed for the product modules in its contract — that is the ceiling on what anyone can do there.

The one rule everywhere: what a person can do at a plant = their permission AND the plant's licensed modules. Licences are edited only on this screen.

    Role library — every role that exists, in one register
    
    
    The complete vocabulary — five base roles and four admin grants, fixed, with live holder counts. There are no custom roles. When someone needs more or less than the standard, the answer is a reasoned per-person exception — never a new role.
