The three screens, on the accepted tree model

Module → Feature → CRUD + named actions · one role per person · grants & exceptions as their own dropdowns · plant licences cap everything. Interactive mockup — CRUD ticks are illustrative, action keys are real.

Design intent — one decision at a time, top to bottom. Directory → pick a person → three account-level fields (role · admin grant · exceptions) → plant access → the permission tree, per plant. The tree is read-mostly: standard ticks come from the role; touching any box becomes an amber override that demands a written reason before save.

Design intent — a read-only register. Pick a role and see its exact tick-pattern over the whole tree (amber actions included; co-sign visibly L4-only). The four grants live on the same screen as their own plane: what each one administers, Full Site shown as the union, Global as the superuser. Nothing here is editable — the catalog is closed.
🔒Hard-locked. Five roles, four grants — nobody can create, rename or edit one (People Admins included). When a role isn't quite right, the answer is a per-person override with a reason, on the person's record.

Design intent — the whole contract on one row. A plant is its name, its cluster label, and its licence chips. Chips are the single write surface (DigitalPaani admin only); every other screen reads them. New plants come through a three-step wizard that commits only at the end, as one audited action.
